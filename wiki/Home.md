# GRAAL analysis wiki

The analysis studies γp → pηπ⁰, with η and π⁰ decaying to four photons. It compares minimum-χ² pairing with a Stage-1 BDT gate followed by the same pairing and missing-mass selection. Calibration and azimuthal statistical inference feed the measured beam asymmetry. The standalone theory package is an explicit downstream calculation.

Start with [installation](getting-started), then [workflow](workflow) and [commands](commands-and-configuration). [Data contracts](data-and-artifacts) explain which inputs and outputs can be combined. [Scientific status](status) records what remains unvalidated as of 23 October 2026.

The numbered components own scientific operations; [architecture](architecture) explains their boundaries. [Plotting](plotting-and-diagnostics), [maintenance and tests](maintenance), and the [finite-bin convention](finite-bin-correction) cover cross-cutting work. The sidebar is the complete page index. This wiki is the current technical guide; dated research under `docs/reference/` is evidence, not a second operating manual.
